About Us

Our Story

Present Minds began developing in the winter of 2025, when Kelsey and Meheret, two high school students at TJHSST, recognized a lack of awareness and understanding surrounding the issue of neurodegenerative diseases and how they affect the elderly. Heavily inspired by organizations such as Neurosphere and Future Female Scholars, they founded Present Minds in order to provide students with the opportunity to learn and engage in research surrounding neurodegenerative diseases.

Our Vision

Our goal is to raise awareness and fundraise for neurodegenerative disease research by providing diverse pathways for student involvement. We will achieve this by offering leadership roles to students, helping them develop informative research articles and publish self-written literary analyses in our seasonal newsletter and internships. In addition, we will organize nursing homes initiatives and spearhead a campaign for a new AP Neuroscience curriculum to be implemented in schools.
